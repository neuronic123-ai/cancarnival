# Can Carnival privacy policy — developer draft

Effective date: complete before publication.
Developer: Vexora Labs.
Contact email: add your chosen support email before publication.

Can Carnival stores game progress, coins, cosmetic selections, achievement statistics and audio settings on the device. No game account is required, and this version has no cloud game-save service. Clearing application data removes locally stored progress.

The Android app uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. Depending on consent, region and configuration, Google may process advertising or device identifiers, technical/diagnostic information and ad interaction information. Google Play Billing processes optional purchases; the game uses purchase status, signed purchase data and purchase tokens to verify and acknowledge paid ad removal. The developer does not receive payment-card information through this game.

Ad privacy options are accessible through the Store when the consent provider requires them. A paid one-time ad-removal purchase disables automatic ads. Optional reward videos remain available only when the player chooses to watch one. A purchase can be restored through the same Google Play account.
