Can Carnival Privacy Policy

Developer: Vexora Labs
Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Can Carnival Android game.

Game progress and settings

The game stores progress, coins, achievements, preferences, and purchase status locally on your device. The game does not require you to create an account.

Advertising

Can Carnival uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google's advertising SDK collects and shares information such as your IP address, device or account identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Your IP address may be used to estimate your general location.

This information is used to provide advertising, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent requirements, your choices, and service settings. Where required, the game presents advertising consent choices.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. You can also manage available advertising privacy controls in your Android device settings.

In-app purchases

The game supports an optional purchase to remove automatic ads through Google Play. Optional rewarded ads may remain available if you choose to watch them. Google Play processes payments; Vexora Labs does not receive your card details. The game uses purchase information to verify and restore purchase benefits.

Contact information

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared. You can remove it through Android's app storage settings. Clearing data resets local progress and preferences. Google handles information collected by its services under its own privacy and retention policies.

Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Some information may be retained where necessary to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about information associated with your child's use of the game, please contact us using the email address below.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
